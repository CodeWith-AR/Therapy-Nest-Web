Clinical Frameworks & Dataset Acknowledgments

  • Western Aphasia Battery–Revised (WAB-R): Clinical subscales (spontaneous speech, comprehension, repetition, naming) provided the taxonomy for our diagnostic assessment.
  • OpenImages Dataset (Google): Exercise visual stimuli utilize CC-BY-4.0 open images curated for cognitive confrontation naming tasks.
  • AphasiaBank / TalkBank: Speech-language rehabilitation corpora informing phonemic similarity calibration.

Offline Practice

Common Inquiries

Commercial therapy apps (such as Constant Therapy) charge $30/month, creating an insurmountable financial barrier for millions of stroke and aphasia survivors globally. Therapy Nest is an open-source non-profit initiative built on local on-device models and free-tier infrastructure to provide free recovery for everyone.

Therapy Nest embeds a compact, pre-trained 50MB Vosk acoustic model. When you speak, your phone's processor transcribes the audio locally in memory without uploading sound files to any cloud server, protecting your medical privacy.

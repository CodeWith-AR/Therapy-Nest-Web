Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 31, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

Summary of Our Core Privacy Promises

  • Voice & Speech Privacy: Your voice recordings are transcribed 100% on your device via Vosk and immediately deleted. We never store or transmit audio recordings to any server.
  • Zero Commercial Advertising: We never sell, rent, or monetize patient data. There are no advertising SDKs or third-party trackers in Therapy Nest.
  • Full Offline Autonomy: Therapy Nest functions completely offline without requiring continuous internet access.
  • Complete Data Control: You can permanently delete your entire account and exercise history at any time directly within the mobile app.

1. Information We Collect

Therapy Nest collects only the minimum information necessary to provide clinically personalized rehabilitation exercises:

  • Account Information: If you choose to register, we collect your email address and an optional display name to synchronize your progress across devices.
  • Clinical Profile Preferences: Conditions selected during onboarding (e.g., stroke, aphasia, TBI), recovery goals, and practice schedule preferences.
  • Therapy Performance Metrics: Numerical assessment scores, response latency (in milliseconds), hint counts, and computed Item Response Theory ability estimates (θ).

2. On-Device Speech Recognition & Audio Data

Therapy Nest includes speech repetition, naming, and reading exercises. When you tap the microphone button:

Microphone audio is captured in real-time and converted to text strictly inside your phone's memory using an on-device Vosk acoustic engine. No raw audio file, voice recording, or audio stream is ever uploaded to Supabase, Firebase, or any cloud server. Once transcription is complete, the audio buffer is purged instantly.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected data solely for:

  • Dynamically adjusting exercise difficulty via our 2PL IRT adaptive engine.
  • Generating your personal progress charts, recovery streaks, and milestone timelines.
  • Sending scheduled daily practice reminders via Firebase Cloud Messaging (if opted in).
  • Aggregated, anonymized clinical research evaluation (only if you provide explicit in-app research consent).

4. Data Storage, Security & Retention

All remote data is stored in PostgreSQL databases managed by Supabase, protected by enterprise-grade Row Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring patients can only access their own records. Data is encrypted using AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.

Offline exercise history is saved in a local SQLite database (via Drift) on your device's private sandbox storage, inaccessible to other applications.

5. Account Deletion & Your Rights

You have full ownership of your data under GDPR, CCPA, and global health data protection standards. You may request account deletion at any time:

  1. Open the Therapy Nest app and tap Settings → Profile → Delete Account.
  2. Confirming deletion immediately cascades and permanently wipes your profile, sessions, ability estimates, and authentication records from both local storage and cloud databases.
  3. Alternatively, email support@therapynest.app with the subject "Account Deletion Request".

6. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Therapy Nest is designed primarily for adults (18+) recovering from stroke, brain trauma, or managing cognitive health. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verified parental consent.
